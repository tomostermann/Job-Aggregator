# Value Proposition

## Target Users

Students looking for internships and apprenticeships,
primarily from engineering schools (Epitech and equivalent),
who are confronted daily with unreliable and poorly filtered
job listings that waste their time.

## Prioritized Pain Points

Out of the 12 pain points identified in the market scan,
we selected 3 based on their frequency and direct impact
on students' job search efficiency:

1. **Ghost Jobs** — Listings remaining online after the position
   is filled, causing students to apply for nothing.
2. **Faulty contract filters** — Impossible to isolate internships
   and apprenticeships from permanent offers on generalist platforms.
3. **School/work schedule incompatibility** — Recruiters almost never
   specify accepted rhythms (e.g. 3 weeks/1 week), leading to wasted
   interviews.

These 3 pain points share a common root: **a lack of reliability
and precision in job listings**, which is something aggregators
like Indeed or LinkedIn do not address.

## Value Proposition

> **A job aggregator built for students, not recruiters.**
> We centralize tech offers from WeLoveDevs and surface only
> what matters to students: verified active listings, contract
> type precision, and schedule compatibility — all filterable
> in under 2 clicks.

| Ghost job detection                | ✗                 | ✓ (14-day flag) |
| Internship / apprenticeship filter | Partial           | ✓ Dedicated     |

## Expected User Value

A student can find a **reliable, compatible, and active offer**
in under 2 minutes, without having to cross-reference
multiple platforms or discover too late that the offer
is outdated or incompatible with their schedule.

## Success Metrics

- Ghost job flag active on offers older than 14 days
  without recruiter activity
- Contract type filter used in > 60% of search sessions
- Schedule-specific filter used in > 50% of apprenticeship searches
- A relevant offer found in under 2 minutes (user testing)
