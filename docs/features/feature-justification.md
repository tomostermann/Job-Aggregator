# Feature Justification

## 1. Ghost Jobs

**Problem**
Advertisements remaining online even though the position has already been filled, causing a major waste of time.

**Hypothesis**
A notification is sent to the recruiter every two weeks so they can update the job posting. If it is not updated, it will be marked as a "Ghost Job." This system helps ensure the reliability of job postings.

**Measure**

- Offer flagged as "Ghost Job" if no recruiter
  activity detected within 14 days of notification
- Reduction of applications sent to inactive offers
  tracked over time
- Ghost job badge visible on the offer card
  without extra navigation

---

## 2. Faulty contract filters

**Problem**
Difficulty in isolating internships and apprenticeships from permanent job offers in traditional search engines.

**Hypothesis**
Centralizing offers with smart filters (contract type : internship, apprenticeships) will reduce search time significantly.

**Measure**

- Students can find a relevant offer in under 2 minutes
- Filter usage rate (internship / apprenticeship) > 60% of sessions

---

## 3. School/Work Schedule Compatibility

**Problem**
Job postings almost never specify the accepted school/work schedules (e.g., 3 weeks/1 week, part-time), which leads to wasted time in interviews for nothing.

**Hypothesis**
A filter has been added to allow users to specify the duration of both the work placement and the school placement if the apprenticeship filter is selected. The same applies to internships; once the filter is selected, another filter appears allowing users to specify the desired internship duration and, if applicable, the start date.

**Measure**

- Users can filter offers by school/work rhythm and internship duration in under 2 clicks
- Schedule-specific filters used in > 50% of apprenticeship-related searches
- Reduction of applications sent to schedule-incompatible offers tracked over time
