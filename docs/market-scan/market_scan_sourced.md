# Market Analysis & Benchmark

## 1. Competitive Landscape

- **LinkedIn / Indeed:** Generalist leaders with massive volume, but polluted by outdated listings and a lack of precise technical details.
  - Source: [ResumeUp.AI via Entrepreneur — "1 in 4 Job Listings on LinkedIn Are Likely 'Ghost Jobs'"](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/one-quarter-of-jobs-posted-online-are-fake-ghost-jobs-study/496683)
  - Source: [The Interview Guys — "Ghost Jobs Exposed"](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/ghost-jobs-exposed/)

---

- **Welcome to the Jungle:** Leader in employer branding, but technology filters are often too limited for engineering profiles.
  - Source: [College Recruiter — "Top 10 Job Platforms for Students and Recent Graduates in 2026"](https://www.collegerecruiter.com/blog/2026/04/12/top-10-job-platforms-for-students-and-recent-graduates-in-2026) — notes that specialized platforms still lack depth for technical filtering 

---

- **Talent.io / Hired:** Selective recruitment platforms, often poorly accessible to internship or junior profiles.
  - Source: [HR Dive — "Entry-Level Jobs Should Be Entry Level"](https://www.hrdive.com/news/entry-level-jobs-should-be-entry-level/814058/) — notes barriers for junior profiles entering selective hiring pipelines 

---

- **Glassdoor:** Essential for salary reviews and company culture insights, but the core job search experience is secondary.
  - Source: [CFO Dive — "Job Seekers Avoid Postings With No Salary Transparency"](https://www.cfodive.com/news/job-seekers-avoid-postings-no-salary-transparency-report-labor-compensation/818192/) — references Glassdoor's role in transparency perception vs. job search utility 

---

- **Adzuna / Jooble:** Aggregator models that centralize multiple sources to fight market fragmentation.
  - Source: [JobCopilot — "10 Best Internship Search Sites for Students in 2026"](https://jobcopilot.com/best-internship-sites/) — documents the need for aggregator models given the number of platforms students must monitor 

---

## 2. User Pain Points

- **Extreme fragmentation:** Students must monitor more than 10 different platforms to not miss any internship or apprenticeship offer.
  - Source: [JobCopilot — "10 Best Internship Search Sites for Students in 2026"](https://jobcopilot.com/best-internship-sites/) — lists 10+ separate platforms students need to consult, including Handshake, WayUp, Zintellect, LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.

---

- **Ghost Jobs:** Listings remaining online even though the position has already been filled, causing a major waste of time.
  - Source: [NOSSA — "Ghost Jobs in 2026: What Are Fake Job Postings and How to Avoid Them"](https://www.nossahq.com/resources/ghost-jobs-in-2025-what-are-fake-job-postings-and-how-to-avoid-them) — 40% of companies posted fake job listings in 2024; 81% of recruiters admit their employers post ghost jobs; 27.4% of all U.S. LinkedIn listings are likely ghost jobs 
  - Source: [Fast Company — "Recruiters Dish on 'Ghost Jobs'"](https://www.fastcompany.com/91425252/recruiters-dish-on-ghost-jobs-why-companies-post-them-and-how-to-outsmart-them) 

---

- **Salary opacity:** Salary information is absent from the majority of offers, yet it is a key data point for students and a required field in our database.
  - Source: [Indeed Hiring Lab — "Salary Transparency Growth Slows but Momentum Continues"](https://www.hiringlab.org/2024/10/23/salary-transparency-growth-slows-but-momentum-continues/) — as of Sept. 2024, only 57.8% of U.S. job postings on Indeed contained salary info, meaning over 40% still don't 



---

- **Faulty contract filters:** Difficulty in isolating internships and apprenticeships from permanent job offers in traditional search engines.
  - Source: [JobCopilot — "10 Best Internship Search Sites for Students in 2026"](https://jobcopilot.com/best-internship-sites/) — notes that even Handshake, the most student-focused platform, has an "overwhelming" interface and that students "may still need to supplement with other niche boards" depending on their major 

---

- **Imprecise tech stack:** Lack of clarity on framework versions or tools used (CI/CD, Cloud), making technical evaluation complex.
  - Source: [IntelligentCV — "75% Of Resumes Get Rejected By ATS — Brutal Truth"](https://www.intelligentcv.app/career/ats-resume-rejection-brutal-truth-hack/) — documents the "AngularJS vs Angular" catastrophe where an ATS filtered for the wrong framework version for 3 months 
  - Source: [The Interview Guys — "Ghost Jobs Exposed"](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/ghost-jobs-exposed/) — notes that tech listings often use vague or template-based descriptions without concrete technical details

---

- **Repetitive processes:** Students must create a new account and re-enter their full profile for every platform they consult.
  - Source: [College Recruiter — "Top 10 Job Platforms for Students and Recent Graduates in 2026"](https://www.collegerecruiter.com/blog/2026/04/12/top-10-job-platforms-for-students-and-recent-graduates-in-2026) — highlights account creation fragmentation across platforms as a barrier for early-career job seekers 

---

- **Lack of feedback:** Complete absence of response after submitting an application on major generalist job portals.
  - Source: [NOSSA — "Ghost Jobs in 2026"](https://www.nossahq.com/resources/ghost-jobs-in-2025-what-are-fake-job-postings-and-how-to-avoid-them) — describes how ghost jobs mean "you never hear anything — not even a rejection" and that this "takes a serious toll on people's mental health and financial stability" 

---

- **The "Fake Junior" syndrome:** Offers categorized as "Internship" or "Junior" but paradoxically requiring 2 to 3 years of prior experience on complex stacks.
  - Source: [HR Dive — "Entry-Level Jobs Should Be Entry Level"](https://www.hrdive.com/news/entry-level-jobs-should-be-entry-level/814058/) — "SHRM cited a study that found 61% of jobs advertised as entry level require more than three years of experience" 
  - Source: [Higher Ed Dive — "Entry-Level Jobs Should Be Entry Level"](https://www.highereddive.com/news/entry-level-jobs-should-be-entry-level/815142/) 

---

- **School/work schedule incompatibility:** Job postings almost never specify accepted school/work rhythms (e.g., 3 weeks/1 week, part-time), leading to wasted time in interviews.
  - Source: [JobCopilot — "10 Best Internship Search Sites for Students in 2026"](https://jobcopilot.com/best-internship-sites/) — notes that filtering options rarely include schedule compatibility for student profiles, unlike graduation year or major 

---

- **Remote-washing:** Offers tagged as "Remote" or "Full Remote" to attract clicks, but whose description reveals only one day per week is actually allowed.
  - Source: [LinkedIn Economic Graph via DailyRemote — "How to Find Remote Jobs in 2026"](https://dailyremote.com/advice/how-to-find-remote-jobs) — remote roles were only 9% of newly listed jobs in late 2024 yet drew 35% of all applications on LinkedIn, creating strong incentives for employers to mislabel postings 
  - Source: [LinkedIn — "How to Identify Remote Job Misrepresentation"](https://www.linkedin.com/top-content/recruitment-hr/navigating-remote-job-markets/how-to-identify-remote-job-misrepresentation/)

---

- **Abusive recruitment processes:** No initial visibility on recruitment steps. Students often discover too late that they must complete an unpaid 3-day technical project before even meeting the team.
  - Source: [CFO Dive — "Job Seekers Avoid Postings With No Salary Transparency"](https://www.cfodive.com/news/job-seekers-avoid-postings-no-salary-transparency-report-labor-compensation/818192/) — "59% of job seekers stated they would be less likely to apply for a job posting that required unpaid assignments or excessive take-home work" 

---

- **The keyword wall (HR filter):** Non-technical recruiters or algorithms automatically reject a candidate if they lack the exact keyword (e.g., "React"), even though the student fully masters equivalent concepts and could adapt within days.
  - Source: [Harvard Business School study cited in The Interview Guys — "The ATS Resume Rejection Myth"](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/ats-resume-rejection-myth/) — "technology may screen out qualified applicants in 88% of companies because they don't match specific search terms" 
  - Source: [Taggd — "Is Your ATS Rejecting Top Talent?"](https://taggd.in/hr-glossary/applicant-tracking-system/) — "over 99.7% of recruiters use keyword filters to search for candidates inside their ATS, with 76.4% filtering by skills"
